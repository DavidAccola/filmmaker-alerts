Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Filmmaker Alerts is a personal app for tracking movies, TV shows, and the filmmakers behind them. This policy describes how the app handles your data.

Data we collect and store locally

All app data is stored locally on your device. This includes:

  • Your watchlist (movies and TV shows you are tracking)
  • Watch status and ratings you assign to content
  • Filmmakers and contributors you follow
  • Notification preferences and app settings
  • Cached movie and TV show data fetched from TMDB

This data never leaves your device except as described below under Google Drive Sync.

Google Drive Sync (optional)

If you choose to enable sync, the app uses the Google Drive API to store a copy of your app data in your Google Drive account. Specifically:

  • What data is accessed: The app reads and writes a single JSON file containing your watchlist, ratings, watch statuses, followed contributors, and preferences. This file is stored in the Application Data folder — a private area of your Google Drive that is not visible in the Drive UI and is accessible only to this app.
  • Why it is accessed: Solely to sync your app data between your devices (e.g., between a Windows PC and an Android phone).
  • What we do not do: We do not access any other files in your Google Drive. We do not read, store, or transmit your data to any server we operate. We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than syncing between your own devices.
  • Data transmission: Data is transmitted directly between your device and Google's servers using HTTPS. We have no intermediate server.

Limited use disclosure

Filmmaker Alerts' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google user data is used only to provide the sync feature described above and for no other purpose.

TMDB

Movie and TV show metadata is fetched from The Movie Database (TMDB). Search queries and content requests are subject to TMDB's privacy policy. We do not send any personally identifying information to TMDB.

Analytics and crash reporting

We do not use any analytics, crash reporting, or tracking services. No usage data is collected.

Data security

All local data is stored in your device's private app storage. Data transmitted to Google Drive is sent over HTTPS. We do not operate any servers that store your data.

Data deletion

To delete all app data:

  • Local data: Uninstall the app, or clear app data in your device settings.
  • Google Drive data: In Google Drive, go to Settings → Manage Apps → Filmmaker Alerts → Delete hidden app data. Alternatively, revoking the app's access in your Google Account permissions will prevent future access.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how Google user data is used, you will be prompted to review and consent to the updated policy before sync continues.
